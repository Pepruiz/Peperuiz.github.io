Perales River Bridge

Comprehensive structural analysis, design, and executive engineering for vehicular and pedestrian bridge in Las Barranquillas

Client Private Developer
Location Las Barranquillas, Madrid
Year 2026
Role Full Structural Design & Project

Project Overview

In this assignment, full technical responsibility for the bridge structure was assumed from preliminary conceptual stages. The scope encompassed the complete engineering development, ranging from geometric definition and finite element structural modeling to the drafting and formal certification of both the Basic Permitting Design and the Detailed Executive Construction Project.
