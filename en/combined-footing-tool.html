Theoretical Foundations & Scope of Analysis

Descriptive Memorandum — Rigid Combined Footings

Verification of ground bearing pressures and stiffness classification based on Eurocode 2 & 7 / Structural Code and the Hetényi beam-on-elastic-foundation (Winkler) model.

1

Analysis Scope and Verification Objectives

The analysis calculates the plan geometry (A, B) and depth (H) required to ensure that ground bearing pressures transmitted by a two-column rigid combined footing remain strictly below allowable soil capacities, preventing structural uplift and guaranteeing rigid body behaviour.

σ_max ≤ 1.25 · σ_adm | σ_mean ≤ σ_adm | σ_min > 0 (no uplift)
2

Relative Rigidity Classification (Hetényi Criterion)

For the planar distribution of soil reactions to be valid, the foundation element must behave as a rigid solid relative to subgrade flexibility. The relative stiffness coefficient over continuous elastic support is computed as:

β = ∜( k_s · B / (4 · E_c · I) ) [m⁻¹] | I = B · H³ / 12

The product of the characteristic elastic length and the total foundation length must satisfy the rigidity criterion:

β · L ≤ π / 2 ≈ 1.5708 → Rigid Element Verified
3

Load Envelope & Global Stability Checks

Four extreme sign combinations for bending moments and transverse shears are evaluated. Safety factors against sliding and overturning are systematically verified:

SF_overturning = (A / 2) / |e_x| ≥ 1.8 | SF_sliding = (N_C · tan δ + c · A · B) / √(ΣV_x² + ΣV_y²) ≥ 1.5
