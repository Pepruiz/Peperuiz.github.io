Philosophy & Approach

Who We Are

Technical structural engineering consultancy specialized in rigorous calculation, computational analysis, and constructability optimization for demanding architectural projects.

Normative Rigor & Advanced Analysis

We are a dedicated team of consulting structural engineers specialized in calculation, sizing, and advanced modeling for residential, industrial, and rehabilitation projects.

At RC Estructuras, we combine strict compliance with Eurocodes and international building codes with finite element analysis (FEA) and parametric design tools, ensuring maximum structural safety with efficient and rational material use.

Site Feasibility & Direct Support

We view engineering consultancy as a close partnership with architects, developers, and general contractors from initial schematic design through on-site technical supervision.

We provide proven, constructible solutions for new developments as well as structural diagnostics, underpinning, and reinforcement of complex historical pathologies—always balancing structural integrity, cost containment, and construction deadlines.

Engineering Services

Comprehensive structural engineering solutions, from concept analysis and detailed calculation to construction site assistance and technical audits.

Building Structural Design

Comprehensive design and calculation of residential, commercial, and industrial structures, optimizing the use of reinforced concrete, structural steel, and timber.

Rehabilitation & Retrofitting

Pathological investigations and structural consolidation for residential and historical buildings, including foundation underpinning, timber framing, and column strengthening.

Foundations & Geotechnics

Geotechnical calculation of shallow and deep foundations (isolated, combined footings, pile caps, and micropiles) and earth retaining cantilever structures.

Structural Steel & BIM

Parametric design, BIM modeling (Revit), and advanced optimization of light and heavy structural steelwork for industrial, commercial, and infrastructure use.
