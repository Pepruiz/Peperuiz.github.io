Load Transfer & Geotechnical Verification

A rigid isolated footing is a shallow foundation element designed to safely spread concentrated column loads onto the supporting stratum. The analytical fundamentals of our checks are summarized below.

1

Total Action Effects at Foundation Base

The column delivers a factored axial load Nd. At the soil contact interface, self-weight of the reinforced concrete footing (density 25 kN/m³) and soil backfill overburden are accounted for:

Self-weight = A · B · H · γconcrete
Total surface load = (qperm + qvar) · A · B + Self-weight
NC = Nd + Total surface load

NC represents the resultant net vertical force transmitted to the ground.

2

Eccentricities & Resulting Base Moments

Applied bending moments at the column base displace the resultant force from the geometrical center. An accidental construction eccentricity emin is superposed unfavorably:

etotal = |Md / NC| + emin

Horizontal shears Vd produce additional base moment due to the footing depth H lever arm:

MC,x = NC · eB + Vd,y · H  |  MC,y = NC · eA + Vd,x · H
3

Ground Contact Stress Distribution

Assuming a rigid footing on an elastic ground response, base stresses follow generalized Navier beam-column formulation:

σ = NC / (A·B) ± 6·MC,x / (B²·A) ± 6·MC,y / (A²·B)

Evaluating pressures at the four corners yields maximum (σmax), mean (σmean), and minimum (σmin) stresses.

4

Bearing Capacity Limits

Against allowable ground bearing pressure σadm, three simultaneous criteria must be satisfied:

σmean ≤ σadm  (Global bearing capacity limit)
σmax ≤ 1.25 · σadm  (Corner peak pressure allowance)
σmin > 0  (Full compression contact / no uplift)
5

Global Stability: Overturning & Sliding

Equilibrium limit states are verified with statutory safety factors:

FSoverturn = Stabilizing Moment / Overturning Moment ≥ 1.80
FSsliding = (NC · tan δ + c' · A·B) / Hd ≥ 1.50

Technical Scope

This application covers geotechnical sizing and stability checks. Complete executive structural design additionally includes internal rebar design:

  • Flexural bottom reinforcement calculation in directions A and B
  • Crack width control and reinforcement anchorage lengths
  • Punching shear capacity verification at column control perimeter
