Case Study · Multi-Family Residential

102 Residential Units in Alcalá de Henares

Structural analysis, slab optimization, and foundation engineering for multi-family residential development

Client Residential Developer
Location Alcalá de Henares, Madrid
Year 2025
Role Structural Engineering & Analysis

Project Overview

Multi-family residential development comprising 102 apartments in Alcalá de Henares, Madrid. The engineering scope included complete three-dimensional finite-element analysis of vertical and lateral actions, slab deflection and reinforcement optimization, and deep foundation engineering using bored cast-in-place piles within heterogeneous alluvial gravels and granular subgrade.
