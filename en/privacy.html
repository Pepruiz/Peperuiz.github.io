Legal Information & Compliance

Privacy Policy

Processing and protection of personal data in compliance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and applicable data protection legislation.

1. Data Controller

Controller: RC Estructuras
Email: ingenieria@rcestructuras.es
Website: https://rcestructuras.es/en/

The controller will process users' personal data in compliance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679, General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and other applicable European and Spanish privacy regulations.

2. Personal Data Collected

Through the website's contact form, the following personal data may be collected:

  • Email address.
  • Information provided directly by the user in the subject or project typology field.
  • Information provided directly by the user in the message or technical inquiry field.

Users are advised not to include sensitive personal information or data not necessary for addressing their technical inquiry.

3. Purpose of Processing

Personal data is processed exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Handling and responding to inquiries submitted through the website.
  • Performing preliminary technical assessments of the proposed project characteristics.
  • Maintaining professional communications arising from the submitted inquiry.
  • Evaluating, where appropriate, potential engineering collaboration or professional services.

Data will not be used for unsolicited newsletters, marketing, or commercial spam.

4. Legal Basis

The legal basis for processing is, as applicable:

a) Pre-contractual measures taken at the request of the data subject, when data is provided to evaluate structural engineering services, technical scopes, or potential collaboration.

b) The consent of the data subject, when required for a specific purpose not covered by pre-contractual steps.

Providing data is voluntary; however, certain basic contact information is required to formulate an adequate technical response.

5. Recipients & Service Providers

Personal data may be processed by technology service providers necessary for website operation, hosting, and communications, acting where applicable as data processors.

These providers include OVHcloud for hosting, infrastructure, and email delivery.

No data transfers to third parties are planned, except under legal obligation or when strictly necessary for technical service delivery.

6. International Data Transfers

Depending on technology providers utilized, certain processing may occur within or outside the European Economic Area. In such cases, processing complies with standard contractual clauses and recognized GDPR safeguards.

7. Data Retention

Personal data will be stored for the duration necessary to manage the inquiry and subsequently for the statutory limitation periods required by applicable legal obligations.

8. Your Rights

Users may exercise their statutory rights under the GDPR:

  • Right of access to personal data.
  • Right to rectification of inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure (right to be forgotten).
  • Right to restriction of processing.
  • Right to object to processing.
  • Right to data portability.

To exercise your rights, you can send an email to: ingenieria@rcestructuras.es

You also hold the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority (such as the Spanish AEPD or your national data protection authority) if you consider that processing infringes privacy regulations.

9. Security

The controller implements appropriate technical and organizational measures to safeguard personal data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure.

10. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect legislative, regulatory, or technical changes. The version published at any time is the effective version.
